Football Life

Let's break it down, step by step.

Part 1: Introduction to the sport
My life was built in the Middle East—born and raised here. Didn't have many contacts, but neither lacked friends. I had some friends who introduced me to a game... which I started, but never tried to stop. We found a place to play it, we found ways to learn it. It was hard... really hard cause I knew nothing about the game, so I watched it play out. And then I saw Ronaldo... 41 now, but was 32 when I was watching him... adapted and showed how he plays. Which made me think, I want to be him. But neither of us knew he would try his best.

Part 2: Learning
I started focusing on the game to get better. As I said in the beginning, it was not easy at all with the physical body I had. I was heavy and laggy with the ball, couldn't do many movements, and I was honestly tired and wanted to just let myself free from the game. But then I realized, what's the point of letting it go? I thought, why did I start to learn if I wanted to stop right in the beginning? Yeah sure, I was fat, slow, and had no sense of game... but that doesn't always last, does it? So I became determined. I stopped letting others' words hurt me or affect me. I played, I just played because I loved to play rather than to show others who I am.

Part 3: Turning point
Once I got used to the game, I started playing and enjoying my time as a player and as someone who was enjoying the game rather than just seeing it as a sport, but as a relief of pressure, stress, and found love for the game. Until I met with an injury on my right ankle which forced me to stop playing football for a whole 3 months. I didn't lose my touch, but I lost the way my shots went... it was horrible, it was like I was starting over. My legs wanted to bend into it... but I can't let it, cause that part of me doesn't exist. I relied on speed and pace and running over pure dribbling. Once I figured it's not possible for me to play with the outside of my boot, I knew... I wouldn't be good for the team. People will hate me for the way I play. But this was the only way for me to enjoy the game I loved since I was a kid, and I just didn't want others' words to end a beautiful story of my life.

Part 4: The ending
Now that I lived and had my moments in football, I decided now that I am growing up, I have responsibilities and work to do... and can't always be out there giving time to my favorite sport. Do I regret that I wasted my time? Absolutely not. Indeed, I wish I could go back and congratulate myself for choosing this sport and making me who I am today. All I have for this game and for the people I played it with is respect and love... which stays no matter how far I go from them or the game. Whenever I get the time, I will always come back to this exact moment and push through everything just like I did, and score for myself and for a future.

"NEVER LET YOURSELF DOWN. IF YOU DO, YOU NEVER SEE WHAT'S AHEAD."
